Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay using UPI?

Enter your payment amount and purpose above. Then either tap Generate QR and scan it with your UPI app, or tap Pay via UPI App to open your app directly with the amount pre-filled.

Which UPI apps are supported?

All apps registered on the UPI network are supported — including Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Amazon Pay, iMobile, and any bank's UPI app.

Can I pay using Google Pay?

Yes! Google Pay is fully supported. You can scan the QR code directly from the Google Pay app, or use the Pay via UPI App button which may automatically open Google Pay on your device.

Can I pay using PhonePe?

Yes! PhonePe is fully supported. Open PhonePe, go to Scan & Pay, and scan the generated QR code. Alternatively, use the Pay via UPI App button.

What if my payment fails?

Don’t worry — if a payment fails, no money is deducted from your account. You can try again after a few minutes. If the issue persists, please contact us on WhatsApp and we will assist you.
